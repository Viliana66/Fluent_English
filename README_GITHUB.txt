FLUENT ENGLISH – NEW, INDEPENDENT APP

GOAL
Natural spoken English for everyday life and work.
The app trains useful whole sentences and common sentence patterns, not isolated vocabulary.

STUDY FLOW
1. Read the Bulgarian prompt.
2. Say the English sentence aloud before revealing it.
3. Reveal the natural English version.
4. Listen once.
5. Repeat the complete sentence twice.
6. Rate it: Again or Got it.

CONTENT
160 carefully curated core speaking cards.

Situations – 96 cards
- Everyday Essentials
- Small Talk
- Help & Clarify
- Plans & Time
- Food & Shopping
- Travel
- Work & Meetings
- Problems & Solutions

Patterns – 64 cards
- Questions
- Time & Tenses
- Polite English
- Linking Ideas

Course
Empty and ready for future daily course material.
Course content is kept separately in course.js.

LEARNING STAGES
0 New
1 Familiar
2 Remembered
3 Solid
4 Fluent
5 Automatic

Got it: +1 stage
Again: -1 stage and the phrase returns soon

Review intervals:
Stage 1: 1 day
Stage 2: 2 days
Stage 3: 4 days
Stage 4: 7 days
Stage 5: 14 days

TECHNICAL DESIGN
Every card has a stable unique ID.
Adding future course cards does not shift existing cards or their progress.
This app uses its own localStorage key: fluent-english-v1.
It is completely separate from English Train.

AUDIO
Full English sentence is spoken.
Default language preference: British English (en-GB).
Normal and slow playback are available.
